# A Response Theory Probe for Learned Stochastic AI Simulators, Tested on Lorenz-63

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06798v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出一种基于线性响应理论和Koopman/Ruelle-Pollicott共振的校准式模态分辨检验，用于评估学习到的随机AI模拟器是否对强迫作出正确响应，并在随机Lorenz-63上发现不变统计保真度与响应保真度可以分离，且训练形式决定模拟器学到哪些性质。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

混沌和随机系统的机器学习仿真器通常通过预测技巧和长期统计量来验证。但这两者都不能证明仿真器对强迫的响应是否正确，而投影和归因研究恰恰依赖于这一性质。线性响应理论使这一点变得可检验：强迫响应通过广义涨落-耗散关系由未受扰动的相关性导出，并可在Koopman生成元的随机Ruelle-Pollicott共振上进行分解。基于Koopmanism Response框架，我们将此转化为一种针对学习代理模型的校准化、模态分辨检验：每个代理模型的 rollout 通过或未通过每一项检查，失败率与真实系统独立实现的失败率进行比较。在随机Lorenz-63这一三变量玩具模型上，我们评估了SINDy、MLP、储备池计算机、神经ODE以及带有学习扩散项的神经SDE，每种方法最多进行80次rollout。一个使用正确函数库的稀疏回归模型以与真实系统一致的通过率通过了每一项检查。不变统计量保真度与响应保真度在两个方向上均出现分离：四分之一的储备池计算机rollout通过了所有不变统计量检查并匹配静态磁化率 $\chi(0)$，但却错误地表征了慢松弛模态；而神经ODE和SDE很少达到不变统计量的底线，但在四分之三的rollout中恢复了这些模态。正如对一个在此由快松弛主导的时间积分量所预期的那样，$\chi(0)$ 无法区分这些情况。对于固定网络，训练形式（单步漂移、流映射或通过积分器的多步训练）决定了它能够正确获得这些性质中的哪些。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Machine-learning emulators of chaotic and stochastic systems are usually validated on forecast skill and long-run statistics. Neither certifies that an emulator responds correctly to forcing, the property that projection and attribution studies rely on. Linear response theory makes this testable: the forced response follows from unperturbed correlations through a generalized fluctuation-dissipation relation, and decomposes over the stochastic Ruelle-Pollicott resonances of the Koopman generator. Building on the Koopmanism Response framework, we turn this into a calibrated, mode-resolved test for learned surrogates: each surrogate rollout passes or fails each check, and failure rates are compared with those of independent realizations of the true system. On stochastic Lorenz-63, a three-variable toy model, we evaluate SINDy, an MLP, a reservoir computer, a neural ODE and a neural SDE with learned diffusion, over up to 80 rollouts each. A sparse-regression model with the correct library passes every check at rates consistent with the true system. Invariant-statistics fidelity and response fidelity dissociate in both directions: a quarter of reservoir-computer rollouts pass every invariant-statistics check and match the static susceptibility $χ(0)$, yet misrepresent the slow relaxation modes, while the neural ODE and SDE rarely meet the invariant-statistics floor but recover those modes in three quarters of rollouts. As expected of a time-integrated quantity dominated here by fast relaxation, $χ(0)$ does not separate these cases. For a fixed network, the training formulation (one-step drift, flow map, or multi-step through the integrator) decides which of these properties it gets right.
