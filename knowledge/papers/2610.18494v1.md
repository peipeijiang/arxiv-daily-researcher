# Task-evoked eye movements as candidate digital biomarkers of depression severity in adolescents and young adults

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18494v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该研究探讨任务诱发的眼动能否作为青少年和年轻成人抑郁症严重程度的可解释行为标志，发现更严重的抑郁与更低的注视分配熵、更少的区域间切换、更长的首次目标进入潜伏期和更低的目标停留比例相关，且三阶段级联分类器在四类分层中表现最佳。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

抑郁症严重程度的客观数字化测量可补充传统症状评估，并促进更及时的临床评价。在此，我们研究了任务诱发的眼动是否能为青少年和年轻成人的抑郁严重程度提供可解释的行为标志物。在这项单中心横断面研究中，155名14至20岁的参与者完成了情绪-空间自由观看任务和记忆引导回忆任务，并根据17项汉密尔顿抑郁评定量表（Hamilton Depression Rating Scale）评分进行分层。我们使用协变量调整的序数趋势模型，在六个任务信息域中检验了19个预先设定的眼动特征。抑郁严重程度越高，与注视分配熵越低（标准化$β=-0.599$）、区域间切换次数越少（$β=-0.526$）、首次目标进入潜伏期越长（$β=0.572$）以及目标注视比例越低（$β=-0.678$；所有错误发现率校正后$q<0.001$）相关。情绪特异性注意偏向测量与严重程度无关，而涉及注视离散度的关联在调整焦虑症状后减弱。在一项纳入44个候选眼动特征和人口学协变量的补充机器学习分析中，三阶段级联分类器取得了最高的四分类分层性能（准确率0.665；宏平均F1值0.670；受试者工作特征曲线下面积0.869），优于单阶段和两阶段方法。总之，这些发现表明，视觉探索和记忆引导注视控制的改变是抑郁严重程度的候选数字生物标志物，而非泛化的负性注意偏向。这些结果支持进一步前瞻性评估任务诱发眼动追踪作为及时严重程度评估和临床决策支持的补充方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Objective digital measures of depression severity could complement conventional symptom assessments and facilitate more timely clinical evaluation. Here, we investigated whether task-evoked eye movements provide interpretable behavioral markers of depression severity in adolescents and young adults. In this single-center, cross-sectional study, 155 participants aged 14--20 years completed emotional-spatial free-viewing and memory-guided recall tasks and were stratified by 17-item Hamilton Depression Rating Scale scores. We examined 19 prespecified eye-movement features across six task-informed domains using covariate-adjusted ordinal-trend models. Greater depression severity was associated with lower gaze-allocation entropy (standardized $β=-0.599$), fewer inter-area switches ($β=-0.526$), longer first target-entry latency ($β=0.572$), and lower target dwell proportion ($β=-0.678$; all false-discovery-rate-adjusted $q<0.001$). Emotion-specific attentional-bias measures were not associated with severity, whereas associations involving fixation dispersion weakened after adjustment for anxiety symptoms. In a complementary machine-learning analysis incorporating 44 candidate eye-movement features and demographic covariates, a three-stage cascade classifier achieved the highest four-class stratification performance (accuracy, 0.665; macro-F1, 0.670; area under the receiver operating characteristic curve, 0.869), outperforming single-stage and two-stage approaches. Together, these findings identify altered visual exploration and memory-guided gaze control as candidate digital biomarkers of depression severity, rather than generalized negative attentional bias. These results support further prospective evaluation of task-evoked eye tracking as a complementary approach for timely severity assessment and clinical decision support.
