# Optimal Rates for Learning with Monotone Adversaries

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个单调的对手观察一个独立同分布的带标签样本，并追加有限个由其自行选择的额外示例，这些示例均被目标假设正确标注。学习器看到的是合并后样本的一个均匀洗牌版本，并根据原始分布进行评分。每个示例都被正确标注，但插入的示例依赖于干净样本，因此合并后的样本并非可交换的。引入该模型的Larsen、Pabbaraju和Shetty证明了，对于VC维为\(d\)的类别，经验风险最小化可达到期望误差\(O((d/n)\log(n/d))\)，并且任何已知的最优学习器都可能偏离最优于PAC学习的\(Θ(d/n)\)速率。他们提出疑问：额外的对数项是这些特定算法的产物，还是缺乏可交换性所固有的结果。

我们证明，这一额外代价在VC维大于1时是固有的。在最坏情况下，对于VC维为\(d\)的类别以及已知的有限插入预算，极小极大期望误差在\(d=1\)时为\(Θ(1/n)\)，在\(d\geq 2\)时为\(Θ((d/n)\log(n/d))\)。同样的速率也适用于以Littlestone维\(d_{\mathrm L}\)替代\(d\)的情形，因此干净的在线到批量速率\(O(d_{\mathrm L}/n)\)同样无法实现。因此，略显反直觉的是，添加正确标注的示例可能使学习难度增加一个对数因子，即使对于在在线学习中具有有限错误界（mistake bound）的类别也是如此。维数为一时的上界由一个简单的不当学习器实现，其分析借鉴了一包含图（one-inclusion graph）背后的留一法论证。我们所有的下界都是初等的，并且来自同一个构造：一个显式的类别和先验分布，其中两个目标假设在具有不可忽略质量的某一点上不同，却产生相同的样本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A monotone adversary observes an i.i.d. labeled sample and appends a finite number of further examples of its choice, every one of them labeled correctly by the target hypothesis. The learner sees a uniform shuffle of the combined sample and is scored on the original distribution. Every example is correctly labeled, but the insertions depend on the clean sample, so the combined sample is not exchangeable. Larsen, Pabbaraju, and Shetty, who introduced this model, showed that empirical risk minimization attains expected error $O((d/n)\log(n/d))$ for classes of VC dimension $d$, and that every known optimal learner can be pushed away from the $Θ(d/n)$ rate, optimal for PAC learning. They asked whether the extra logarithm is an artifact of those particular algorithms or an inherent consequence of the lack of exchangeability.
  We show that this additional cost is inherent beyond VC dimension one. In the worst case over classes of VC dimension $d$ and over known finite insertion budgets, the minimax expected error is $Θ(1/n)$ at $d=1$ and $Θ((d/n)\log(n/d))$ for $d\geq 2$. The same rates hold with Littlestone dimension $d_{\mathrm L}$ in place of $d$, so the clean online-to-batch rate $O(d_{\mathrm L}/n)$ is unattainable as well. Thus, somewhat counterintuitively, adding correctly labeled examples can make learning harder by a logarithmic factor, even for classes that admit finite mistake bounds in online learning. The dimension-one upper bound is achieved by a simple improper learner whose analysis adapts the leave-one-out argument underlying the one-inclusion graph. All of our lower bounds are elementary and come from a single construction: an explicit class and prior on which two target hypothesis, which differ a point of nonnegligible mass, produce the same sample.
