# Sample More, Reflect Less: Self-Refine and Reflexion Lose to Repeated Sampling at Equal Token Cost, from 1.5B to 7B

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28576v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文通过实验证明，在相同token成本下，重复采样比自我反思等自我改进方法更可靠，且自我检查方法在较大模型上表现更差。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

使语言模型规划、批评并重写自身答案、反思错误、从多次尝试中挑选最佳结果，或与自身副本进行辩论的方法，几乎都会使其生成比单条思维链多得多的文本。由于生成更多文本本身就能提高准确率，因此相对于单条思维链的增益并不能证明该方法的核心理念起到了作用。Wang等人（2024）报告称，一个简单的基线方法——对同一问题反复采样并保留最常出现的答案——在预算相当的情况下往往能胜出，但该研究仅给出了点估计，未提供置信区间或显著性检验。

我们将该比较重新设计为一项受控实验：七种方法，开放模型参数规模为1.5B、3B和7B，两个数学基准，每个基准150道题。我们统计每一个生成的词元（token），包括用于批评、反思、辩论轮次和检查的词元，并将每种方法与其自身实测成本下的重复采样进行比较。全部36项比较均按问题配对，并采用自助法（bootstrap）置信区间和多重性校正。

在同等成本下，没有任何方法能稳定优于重复采样。有十种方法稳定劣于重复采样，且全部属于模型检查自身输出的方法；所有18项自我检查比较均为负向结果。随着模型规模增大，两类自我检查方法的表现出现分化。选择机制不再有害：在1.5B规模下，取Best-of-N的八个样本并仅统计最常出现的答案，比让模型自行挑选高出8.0和11.3个百分点；但在7B规模下仅高出2.0和1.3个百分点，已无法与零区分。重写机制无法挽回劣势：在7B规模下，Self-Refine和强制版Reflexion仍比基线低3.6至10.1个百分点。

按原文发表的Reflexion在最小模型上从未触发其自身的重试机制。它每次都判定自己正确，并悄然退化为单条思维链。我们公开了代码、提示词、全部生成结果以及验证脚本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Methods that make a language model plan, criticise and rewrite its own answer, reflect on mistakes, pick the best of several attempts, or debate with copies of itself nearly all make it generate far more text than a single chain of thought. Because generating more text raises accuracy by itself, a gain over one chain of thought does not show the method's idea is what helped. Wang et al. (2024) reported that a simple baseline, sampling the same question repeatedly and keeping the most common answer, often wins once budgets are comparable, but gave point estimates with no confidence intervals or significance tests.
  We rerun that comparison as a designed experiment: seven methods, open models of 1.5B, 3B and 7B parameters, two mathematics benchmarks, 150 questions each. We count every generated token, including those spent on critiques, reflections, debate turns and checking, and compare each method against repeated sampling at its own measured cost. All 36 comparisons are paired by question, with bootstrap intervals and multiplicity correction.
  No method is reliably better than repeated sampling at equal cost anywhere. Ten are reliably worse, all of them methods where the model inspects its own output, and all 18 self-inspection comparisons are negative. The two kinds of self-inspection part company as models grow. Choosing stops hurting: taking Best-of-N's eight samples and just counting the most common answer beats letting the model pick by 8.0 and 11.3 points at 1.5B, but only 2.0 and 1.3 at 7B, no longer distinguishable from zero. Rewriting does not recover: Self-Refine and a forced Reflexion stay 3.6 to 10.1 points below baseline at 7B.
  Reflexion as published never triggered its own retry on the smallest model. It judged itself correct every time and silently became a single chain of thought. We release code, prompts, all generations, and our verification scripts.

</details>
