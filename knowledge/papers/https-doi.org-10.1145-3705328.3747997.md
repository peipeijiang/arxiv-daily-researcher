<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

新闻推荐系统是现代数字信息生态系统中不可或缺的组成部分，帮助用户在每日海量内容中高效导航。近年来，生成式人工智能（尤其是大语言模型）的出现，通过实现自动化内容生成、多模态分析和对话式交互，深刻重塑了新闻领域的格局。尽管这些进展带来了重大机遇，但也引发了关于模型漂移、事实不一致性以及人工智能生成错误信息可能加剧两极分化并侵蚀公众信任的担忧。此外，不断演进的监管环境对算法策展的透明度、问责制和公平性提出了新的要求。第十三届新闻推荐与分析国际研讨会（INRA）为来自不同学科的研究人员和从业者提供了一个专门的平台，用以交流思想、探讨技术发展，以及新闻个性化与分析在法律、伦理和社会层面的影响。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

News recommender systems are integral to the modern digital information ecosystem, helping users navigate the vast volume of daily content. The recent advent of Generative AI, particularly Large Language Models, has profoundly reshaped the news landscape by enabling automated content generation, multi-modal analysis, and conversational interfaces. Although these advancements offer significant opportunities, they also raise concerns regarding model drift, factual inconsistencies, and the potential for AI-generated misinformation to fuel polarization and erode public trust. Moreover, the evolving regulatory environment imposes new requirements for transparency, accountability, and fairness of algorithmic curation. The 13th International Workshop on News Recommendation and Analytics (INRA) serves as a dedicated venue for researchers and practitioners from various disciplines to exchange ideas and discuss technical developments, as well as the legal, ethical, and societal implications of news personalization and analytics.
