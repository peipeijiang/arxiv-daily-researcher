<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在线劳动力市场在连接全球自由职业者与雇主方面发挥着至关重要的作用。尽管现有方法大多侧重于帮助雇主找到最佳候选人，但针对以自由职业者为中心的方法的研究相对较少。在本研究中，我们提出了一种以自由职业者为中心的技能顾问（FESA），该顾问结合了竞争力评估器和优化模型。竞争力评估器将最先进的深度学习推荐系统与对比学习相结合，以计算自由职业者与其期望任务之间的匹配分数，而优化模型则规定自由职业者应获取哪些技能，以提高其在这些任务中的竞争力，同时使学习过程可控。FESA还支持两种再技能策略：探索和利用。我们使用从一家领先在线劳动力市场收集的大规模真实世界数据集，将FESA与替代推荐模型进行了评估。我们还开展了一项有人类参与者参与的对照实验，以考察真实世界用户如何回应FESA推荐，以及披露自由职业者的排名——FESA的一项关键输出——是否会影响再技能动机。与传统组织情境中的先前研究发现——即排名信息通常会鼓励更大努力——相反，我们发现披露自由职业者的低排名会显著降低其再技能动机。这种排名披露的消极激励效应在探索式再技能策略下比在利用式再技能策略下更为明显，前者要求自由职业者获取更具挑战性和不熟悉的技能，而后者则建议自由职业者获取与其现有专长更紧密契合的技能。我们的研究强调，设计以自由职业者为中心的推荐系统不仅需要算法创新，还需要仔细关注系统设计如何影响用户动机和参与度。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Online labor markets play a vital role in connecting freelancers and employers worldwide. While most existing methods focus on helping employers find the best candidates, relatively little research addresses freelancer-centric approaches. In this study, we propose a freelancer-centric skill advisor (FESA) that combines a competitiveness evaluator and an optimization model. The competitiveness evaluator integrates a state-of-the-art deep learning recommender system with contrastive learning to calculate matching scores between freelancers and their desired tasks, while the optimization model prescribes skills a freelancer should acquire to improve their competitiveness for those tasks while making the learning experience manageable. FESA also supports two reskilling strategies: exploration and exploitation. We evaluate FESA against alternative recommendation models using a large-scale, real-world dataset collected from a leading online labor market. We also conduct a controlled experiment with human participants to examine how real-world users respond to FESA recommendations and whether disclosing a freelancer’s ranking, a key output of FESA, affects reskilling motivation. In contrast to prior findings from traditional organizational settings, where ranking information generally encourages greater effort, we find that disclosing a freelancer’s low ranking significantly reduces motivation to reskill. This demotivating effect of ranking disclosure is more pronounced under the exploration reskilling strategy, which requires freelancers to acquire more challenging and unfamiliar skills, than under the exploitation reskilling strategy where freelancers are advised to acquire skills that are more closely aligned with their existing expertise. Our research highlights that designing a freelancer-centric recommendation system requires not only algorithmic innovation but also careful attention of how system design influences user motivation and engagement.
