# The Geometry of Language Model Logits: Generic Structure and Learned Departures

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18520v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文系统分析了自回归语言模型词表 logits 的几何结构，发现有序 logits 在不同上下文、架构和规模下遵循 log-linear 关系，且训练后斜率 α 从约 0.2 升至 1.4–1.7 并与预测性能相关，但该工作与推荐系统领域无关。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

自回归语言模型在每一个预测步骤都会产生一个词汇表大小的 logit 向量，其结构决定了条件下一词元的分布。我们通过实证发现，有序的词汇表头部 logit 遵循一种显著一致的 log-线性关系
  \[ z_{(r)} \approx μ- α\log r. \] 这一关系在预测上下文、架构和规模上均成立，并在预训练过程中逐步显现。据我们所知，这是对先前解码工作中所用分布假设背后的 logit 几何结构的首次系统分析。尽管涵盖有界、轻尾和重尾分布的受控顺序统计基线可以在很大程度上解释观测到的 log-线性几何结构，但它们无法解释斜率 $α$ 的系统性偏移：训练后的语言模型表现出 $α\approx 1.4$--$1.7$，远高于初始化基线（$α\approx 0.2$）。此外，$α$ 与预测性能相关，表明它捕捉到了超越一般极值效应的学习结构。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Autoregressive language models produce, at every prediction step, a vocabulary-sized logit vector whose structure determines the conditional next-token distribution. We empirically identify that the ordered vocabulary-head logits follow a remarkably consistent log-linear relation
  \[ z_{(r)} \approx μ- α\log r. \] across prediction contexts, architectures, and scales, emerging progressively during pretraining. To our knowledge, this is the first systematic analysis of the logit geometry underlying the distributional assumptions used in the prior decoding work. While controlled order-statistic baselines spanning bounded, light-tailed, and heavier-tailed distributions can largely explain the observed log-linear geometry, they do not explain the systematic shift in the slope $α$: trained language models exhibit $α\approx 1.4$--$1.7$, sitting well above the initialization baseline ($α\approx 0.2$) . Moreover, $α$ correlates with predictive performance, suggesting that it captures learned structure beyond generic extreme-value effects.

</details>
