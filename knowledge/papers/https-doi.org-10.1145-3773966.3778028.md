数十年来，WSDM（网络搜索与数据挖掘）学界一直通过协同过滤与预测性行为模型的视角来审视个性化技术。如今，我们正见证一场根本性的转变——人工智能系统不再仅仅预测用户意图，而是能够主动推理、规划并代我们进行创造。这些新方法不再只是强化我们已有的习惯，而是有望揭示我们视而不见的洞见，引导我们走向那些以新颖且出人意料的方式推进个人旅程的发现。然而，我们是否真的正处于超级智能个性化时代的边缘？

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

For decades, the WSDM community has viewed personalization through the lens of collaborative filtering and predictive behavioral models. Today, we are witnessing a fundamental shift where AI systems promise to not just predict user intent, but to actively reason, plan, and create on our behalf. Instead of merely reinforcing our existing habits, these new approaches promise to surface insights we are blind to, guiding us toward discoveries that advance our personal journeys in new and unexpected ways. But are we truly on the verge of super-intelligent personalization?
