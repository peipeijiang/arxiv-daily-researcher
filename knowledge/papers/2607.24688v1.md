# Beyond Scale and Generation: Understanding Language Model-based Entity Matching

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24688v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文通过控制实验研究语言模型在实体匹配任务中不同架构（bi-encoder、cross-encoder、生成式）的性能差异，发现模型变体、架构选择和分布偏移是关键因素，生成式匹配器并非普遍优于交叉编码器。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

实体匹配识别指向同一真实世界实体的记录。语言模型可通过双编码器、交叉编码器和生成式匹配器架构适配于此任务。然而，先前的研究常将匹配器架构与模型主干、模型变体（反映不同预训练目标）及模型规模差异混为一谈，导致难以分离性能提升的来源。我们通过一项受控因子研究解决了这一问题，该研究涵盖Qwen3系列的三种匹配器架构、三种模型变体与三种模型规模，以及九个数据集，总计1,215次微调运行。我们还评估了跨数据集迁移性与计算成本。

研究结果表明，模型变体对双编码器至关重要：面向嵌入的变体提供了更强的初始化能力与更优的表征几何结构，可预测下游匹配性能。交叉编码器相较于双编码器保持了一致优势，因其联合编码记录对而非独立表征每条记录，尽管更大规模的模型部分缩小了这一差距。生成式匹配器并未普遍优于交叉编码器，其优势集中于分布偏移场景，包括记录模式中细微的未见差异及跨数据集迁移。我们进一步发现，更大规模的模型更依赖捷径学习，因此未必表现更优。这些发现厘清了不同匹配器架构性能差异的潜在因素，并推动未来研究与基准设计更清晰地区分架构选择与模型层面因素，同时明确评估分布偏移与跨数据集迁移性。我们在https://github.com/Jantory/llm-trained-matcher 公开了实验结果、代码、训练脚本及评估数据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Entity matching identifies records that refer to the same real-world entity. Language models can be adapted to this task through bi-encoder, cross-encoder, and generative matcher architectures. However, prior studies often conflate matcher architecture with differences in model backbone, model variant(reflecting different pretraining objectives), and model size, making it difficult to isolate the sources of performance gains. We address this issue through a controlled factorial study spanning three matcher architectures, three model variants and three model sizes from the Qwen3 family, and nine datasets, totaling 1,215 fine-tuning runs. We also evaluate cross-dataset transferability and computational cost.
  Our results show that model variant is critical for bi-encoders: embedding-oriented variants provide stronger initialization and more favorable representation geometry predictive of downstream matching performance. Cross-encoders retain a consistent advantage over bi-encoders because they jointly encode record pairs rather than representing each record independently, although larger models partially narrow this gap. Generative matchers do not universally outperform cross-encoders. Instead, their advantages concentrate under distribution shift, including subtle unseen differences in record schemas and cross-dataset transfer. We further find that larger models rely more heavily on shortcut learning and therefore do not necessarily perform better. These findings clarify the factors underlying performance differences across matcher architectures and motivate future research and benchmark designs that better disentangle architectural choices from model-level factors while explicitly evaluating distribution shift and cross-dataset transferability. We release our experimental results, code, training scripts, and evaluation data at https://github.com/Jantory/llm-trained-matcher.
