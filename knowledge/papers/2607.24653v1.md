<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们介绍Kimi K3，这是一个拥有2.8万亿参数、1040亿激活参数的混合专家模型，具备原生视觉能力，并支持100万token的上下文窗口。Kimi K3基于Kimi Delta注意力机制（Kimi Delta Attention）和注意力残差（Attention Residuals）构建，这些技术改善了跨序列长度和模型深度的信息流动。结合稳定潜在混合专家（Stable LatentMoE）——该机制在每个token上有效激活896个路由专家中的16个——以及经过优化的训练和数据方案，这些进展使得整体扩展效率相较于Kimi K2提升了约2.5倍。后训练阶段突出了在通用、智能体及编程领域的强化学习，以及多种推理努力级别，从而实现了组合泛化与稳健的长周期执行。在2.8万亿规模下，Kimi K3得到了多个领域基础设施进展的支持：面向KDA的算法-系统协同设计、具备高效内存管理的完美均衡专家并行训练、带有持续回滚与沙盒状态的百万token智能体强化学习，以及部署创新。大量评估表明，Kimi K3在长周期编程、智能体、知识、推理及视觉任务上均达到了前沿水平。尽管其整体性能仍落后于最强大的专有模型，即Claude Fable 5和GPT-5.6 Sol，但Kimi K3在我们的评估套件中持续优于其他开源及专有模型。我们开放了完整的Kimi K3模型权重，以促进未来研究，并加速前沿智能的更广泛部署与应用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce Kimi K3, a 2.8T parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 104 billion activated parameters, native vision capabilities, and a 1-million-token context window. Kimi K3 is built on Kimi Delta Attention and Attention Residuals, which improve information flow across sequence length and model depth. Together with Stable LatentMoE, which effectively activates 16 of 896 routed experts per token, and refined training and data recipes, these advances yield an approximately 2.5x improvement in overall scaling efficiency over Kimi K2. Post-training highlights reinforcement learning across general, agentic, and coding domains and multiple reasoning-effort levels, enabling compositional generalization and robust long-horizon execution. At 2.8T scale, Kimi K3 is supported by infrastructure advances in multiple areas: algorithm-system co-design for KDA, perfectly balanced expert-parallel training with efficient memory management, million-token agentic RL with persistent rollout and sandbox states, and deployment innovations. Extensive evaluations show that Kimi K3 achieves frontier-level performance across long-horizon coding, agentic, knowledge, reasoning, and vision tasks. While its overall performance still trails the most powerful proprietary models, namely Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, Kimi K3 consistently outperforms other open and proprietary models evaluated in our suite. We release the full Kimi K3 model weights to facilitate future research and accelerate the broader deployment and adoption of frontier intelligence.
