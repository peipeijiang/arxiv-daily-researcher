# How Low Can You Go: Fine-Tuning LLMs with Bayesian Optimization (LlMBO)

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18504v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

最近，Morris等人提出了TinyLoRA，一种用于大语言模型（LLM）微调的策略，它引入任意少的参数，并展示了使用不到10个参数即可实现准确率提升，而基于LoRA的方法通常需要约10,000个以上参数。然而，该研究仍然沿用了一开始用于训练数十亿参数LLM的那种基于梯度的局部搜索方法。本文基于这样一个观察：在低维设定下，全局优化方法变得可行，从而开启了大语言模型贝叶斯优化（Large language Model Bayesian Optimization, LlMBO）。LlMBO样本高效、无梯度、内存占用低、可跨设备并行且无需同步，并且与策略梯度方法不同，它可以直接优化贪心解码准确率。我们描述了数值实验，在数学基准套件上针对多种模型的微调任务中比较LlMBO与GRPO，结果表明LlMBO在这一情形下可以成为一种合适的替代方案，达到或超过GRPO的微调增益，同时只使用其一小部分计算和内存。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Recently, Morris et al. introduced TinyLoRA, a strategy for LLM fine-tuning which introduces arbitrarily few parameters, and demonstrated accuracy improvements using less than $10$ parameters rather than the ${\gtrsim 10,000}$ typically demanded by LoRA-based methods. However, that study continued to use gradient-based local search methods of the kind deployed for training multi-billion parameter LLMs in the first place. This article is predicated on the observation that, in the low dimensional setting, global optimization methods become tractable, unlocking Large language Model Bayesian Optimization (LlMBO). LlMBO is sample-efficient, gradient-free, memory-light, parallelizes across devices without synchronization, and, unlike policy-gradient methods, can optimize greedy-decoding accuracy directly. We describe numerical experiments comparing LlMBO against GRPO on fine-tuning tasks for a variety of models on a math benchmark suite which show that LlMBO can be a suitable alternative in this regime, matching or exceeding GRPO's fine tuning gains, while using a fraction of the compute and memory.
