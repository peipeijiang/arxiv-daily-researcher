# Direct Intermediate Initialization for Tilted Diffusion Samplers

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06834v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文针对扩散后验采样器MCGDiff提出直接中间初始化方法，通过高斯桥将软化干净空间后验样本映射到倾斜目标并仅运行剩余SMC后缀，在结构化高斯混合逆问题和稀有模态问题上显著提升了有限粒子采样性能。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一些扩散后验采样器在反向过程中构造高斯倾斜的中间分布。我们观察到，这些目标可以通过较弱条件化拉回到干净空间后验，并通过高斯桥将样本解析地传输到相应的噪声空间目标。对于序贯蒙特卡洛（SMC）采样器MCGDiff，这一拉回问题的有效观测方差最高可达扩散噪声方差的两倍。我们利用这一结构，将MCGDiff直接初始化在中间时刻：一个近似求解器采样软化后的干净空间后验，高斯桥将这些样本映射到倾斜目标，并且只运行剩余的SMC后缀。这以渐近一致性换取了有限粒子性能。以矩匹配后验采样（MMPS）作为求解器时，该混合方法在一个结构化高斯混合逆问题上，在相同粒子数下将切片Wasserstein距离改善了约$2\times$，而当后验相关模态在先验下稀有之时，改善超过一个数量级。一个先验初始化对照实验保留了桥但去掉了干净空间条件化，表明在MCGDiff的标准高斯混合基准上，大部分改进对条件化不敏感。对初始化进行条件化在结构化问题上带来了进一步的一致增益，并在稀有模态问题上变得决定性，因为重采样无法重新填充初始种群中不存在的模态。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Some diffusion posterior samplers construct Gaussian-tilted intermediate distributions along the reverse process. We observe that these targets can be pulled back to clean-space posteriors with weaker conditioning, with samples transported analytically to the corresponding noisy-space target through a Gaussian bridge. For the sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) sampler MCGDiff, the effective observation variance of this pulled-back problem is up to twice the diffusion-noise variance. We exploit this structure to initialize MCGDiff directly at an intermediate time: an approximate solver samples the softened clean-space posterior, the Gaussian bridge maps these samples to the tilted target, and only the remaining SMC suffix is run. This trades asymptotic consistency for finite-particle performance. With moment-matching posterior sampling (MMPS) as the solver, the hybrid improves sliced Wasserstein distance by roughly $2\times$ at matched particle count on a structured Gaussian-mixture inverse problem, and by more than an order of magnitude when the posterior-relevant mode is rare under the prior. A prior-initialization control, which retains the bridge but drops the clean-space conditioning, shows that on MCGDiff's standard Gaussian-mixture benchmark most of the improvement is insensitive to the conditioning. Conditioning the initialization gives a further consistent gain on the structured problem, and becomes decisive on a rare-mode problem, where resampling cannot repopulate a mode absent from the initial population.
