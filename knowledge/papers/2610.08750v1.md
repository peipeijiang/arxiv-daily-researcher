<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Petri网已被用于描述化学反应等化学过程。它与化学具有很好的映射关系：库所（Place）是原子之间的键以及每个原子的自由价，令牌（token）是键级的一个单位，变迁（transition）形成或断裂一个键，守恒量是原子的价预算，而使能规则是价规则。这些语义并不能由学习到的反应模型或建立在Petri网之上的神经网络所保证——后者仅将网作为消息传递的脚手架。在此，我们追问：什么样的架构对其权重的每一个取值都仍然是一个Petri网。我们在理论中找到了答案：一个网的所有语义共享激发形式 $m^\prime=m+Cσ$、局部性（因为使能仅读取一个变迁的输入）以及使能规则；我们证明了守恒性强制了激发形式，非负性在局部速率定律上强制了使能规则。这使得速率定律——即每个变迁激发的倾向——保持自由。我们引入了Neural Petri Flow（NPF），它学习该速率定律，或用于分类的读出，并将其余部分硬编码为无参数层。在我们称之为价网（valence net）的网络上，原子映射、反应分类和前向预测成为同一激发向量上的三个任务。在无需训练的情况下，最小激发向量在精心整理的Golden集上映射了88.8%，而RXNMapper为85.6%；在EnzymeMap的酶促反应上为88.7%对77.9%。在USPTO-480K上，在这些激发向量上训练的NPF预测了87.7%的产物，而在训练反应的1%子集上训练时为67.4%。ECREACT的EC编号在第三层级上对90.2%的反应被预测正确，领先最佳已发表方法5.6个百分点。以电子作为令牌，相同的令牌博弈首先预测了FlowER中90.5%的基元步骤，领先已发表的基线，并且每一个top-1预测都是无需过滤的合法分子。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Petri nets have been used to describe chemical processes such as reactions.They map well to chemistry: Places are the bonds between atoms and the free valence of each atom, a token is a unit of bond order, a transition forms or breaks a bond, the conserved quantities are the valence budgets of the atoms, and the enabling rule is the valence rule. These semantics are not guaranteed by learned models of reactions or neural networks that are built on Petri nets that use the net as a scaffold for message passing. Here, we ask what architecture remains a Petri net for every value of its weights. We find the answer in the theory, where all semantics of a net share the firing form $m^\prime=m+Cσ$, locality, as enabling reads only the inputs of a transition, and the enabling rule, and we prove that conservation forces the firing form and that non-negativity forces the enabling rule on local rate laws. This leaves free the rate law, which is the propensity of each transition to fire. We introduce Neural Petri Flow, which learns this rate law, or a readout for classification, and hard-wires the rest as parameter-free layers. On what we denote a valence net, atom mapping, reaction classification, and forward prediction become three tasks on one firing vector. Without training, the minimum firing vector maps 88.8% of the curated Golden set against 85.6% for RXNMapper, and 88.7 against 77.9% of the enzymatic reactions of EnzymeMap. On USPTO-480K, NPF trained on these firing vectors predicts 87.7% of the products and 67.4% when trained on a 1% subset of the training reactions. EC numbers of ECREACT are predicted at the third level for 90.2% of reactions, 5.6 points ahead of the best published method. With electrons as tokens, the same token game predicts 90.5% of the elementary steps of FlowER first, ahead of the published baseline, and every top-1 prediction is a valid molecule without a filter.
