<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

电子商务搜索与推荐领域是信息检索（IR）中一个独特且动态的领域，其特点在于多模态性和行业驱动的挑战。虽然满足用户信息需求的基本任务与网络搜索一致，但所采用的方法却截然不同。在电子商务平台（如阿里巴巴、亚马逊、eBay、Etsy、Flipkart、沃尔玛）上，可用于检索和排序的数据存在显著差异，成功信号（如将商品加入购物车、购买）也各不相同。我们2025年的重点是通过互动讨论促进更深层次的参与，探索关键议题，例如应对研究到产品管线中的不可复现性问题，以及探讨新兴主题，如大语言模型（LLM）的评估指标、多模态性，以及自然搜索与付费搜索之间的相互作用。凭借我们以讨论为主的形式和结构化的引导，我们旨在激发所有参与者的对话与交流。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The eCommerce search and recommendations space is a unique and dynamic domain within information retrieval (IR), characterized by its multimodality and industry-driven challenges.While the basic task of fulfilling a user's information need aligns with web search, the methodologies employed are distinct.On eCommerce platforms (e.g.Alibaba, Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Flipkart, Walmart), the data available for retrieval and ranking differs significantly, as do the success signals (e.g.adding items to a cart, purchasing).Our focus for 2025 is on fostering deeper engagement through interactive discussions, exploring crucial topics such as navigating irreproducibility in research-to-product pipelines, and addressing emerging topics such as evaluation metrics for LLMs, multimodality, and the interplay between organic and sponsored search.With our discussion-heavy format and structured facilitation, we aim to spark conversation among all participants.
