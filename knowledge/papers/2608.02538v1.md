# Interaction Is Not Necessary for Order-Optimal 1-Bit Mean Estimation

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究一位均值估计问题，其中每个独立样本仅由一个二进制消息表示。我们考虑定义在$\mathbb{R}$上、均值位于$[-λ,λ]$且绝对$k$阶中心矩至多为$σ^k$的分布，其中$k>1$固定。对于这一分布类，先前的工作通过两阶段协议在一般查询下达到了最优样本复杂度。第一阶段对均值进行定位。第二阶段查询在定位之后选择，并围绕解码中心细化估计。我们证明，通过构造一个随机化的完全非自适应协议，可以在观测数据之前固定所有查询，并达到与最优自适应协议相同的样本复杂度，从而避免这种交互。对于目标精度$ε$和置信度$1-δ$，其样本复杂度为 \[ \log\fracλσ +
\begin{cases} (σ/ε)^2\log(1/δ), & k>2,\\ (σ/ε)^2\log(σ/ε)\log(1/δ), & k=2,\\ (σ/ε)^{k/(k-1)}\log(1/δ), & 1<k<2, \end{cases} \] 其中常数仅依赖于$k$。在已知下界覆盖的范围内，该速率即使在完全自适应协议中也是极小极大最优的。这为COLT 2026开放问题给出了否定答案，该问题询问在一般查询下，交互对于阶最优的一位均值估计是否必要 \citep[开放问题~1]{lau2026open}。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper is concerned with one-bit mean estimation, where each independent sample is represented by a single binary message. We consider distributions on $\mathbb{R}$ with mean in $[-λ,λ]$ and absolute $k$-th central moment at most $σ^k$, where $k>1$ is fixed. For this class, previous work attained the optimal sample complexity for general queries using a two-stage protocol. The first stage localizes the mean. The second-stage queries are chosen after localization and refine the estimate around the decoded center. We show that this interaction can be avoided by constructing a randomized fully non-adaptive protocol that fixes all queries before observing the data and matches the optimal adaptive sample complexity. For target accuracy $ε$ and confidence $1-δ$, its sample complexity scales as \[ \log\fracλσ +
\begin{cases} (σ/ε)^2\log(1/δ), & k>2,\\ (σ/ε)^2\log(σ/ε)\log(1/δ), & k=2,\\ (σ/ε)^{k/(k-1)}\log(1/δ), & 1<k<2, \end{cases} \] up to constants depending only on $k$. In the range covered by the known lower bound, this rate is minimax optimal even among fully adaptive protocols. This gives a negative answer to the COLT 2026 open problem asking whether interaction is necessary for order-optimal one-bit mean estimation with general queries \citep[Open Problem~1]{lau2026open}.
