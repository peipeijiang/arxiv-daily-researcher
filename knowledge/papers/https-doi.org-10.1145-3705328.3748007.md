<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）的最新突破为开发超越传统推荐方法的智能体推荐系统铺平了道路。本教程介绍智能体推荐系统的关键概念和架构。我们讨论规划、记忆和多模态推理等智能体能力如何实现主动推荐策略，同时应对可解释性、安全性和终身个性化方面的挑战。本课程面向对下一代智能推荐系统感兴趣的学术研究人员和行业从业者。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Recent breakthroughs in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have paved the way for developing agentic recommender systems that go beyond traditional recommendation methods. This tutorial introduces the key concepts and architectures of agentic recommender systems. We discuss how agentic capabilities such as planning, memory, and multimodal reasoning enable proactive recommendation strategies while addressing challenges in explainability, safety, and lifelong personalization. The session is designed for both academic researchers and industry practitioners interested in the next generation of intelligent recommender systems.
