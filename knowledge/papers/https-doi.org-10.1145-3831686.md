可复现性已成为可信推荐系统研究的基石，这源于人们对实验结果可靠性和可推广性的日益关注。为此，ACM RecSys会议于2020年设立了专门的“可复现性轨道”（Reproducibility Track），以鼓励严谨、透明且可重复的研究。本文对该轨道2020至2025年间收录的51篇论文进行了结构化分析。我们按贡献类型对论文进行分类，并分析其在数据集、算法、框架和评估实践方面的常见模式，旨在理解可复现性在社区实践中是如何被具体落实的。研究结果揭示了三个主要趋势。第一，该轨道的范围不断扩大，从最初聚焦于复现和复制，逐步扩展到基准测试、资源和方法论贡献。第二，可复现性论文呈现出一致的方法论特征，依赖于有限的数据集、算法和评估协议。第三，实践中的可复现性往往涉及对先前实验的扩展而非严格复制，研究常常引入额外的模型或评估标准。总体而言，可复现性工作提升了透明度和文档规范性，但对方法论多样性影响有限，这凸显了可复现性的概念定义与其实际实施之间的差距。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Reproducibility has become a cornerstone of credible recommender systems research, driven by growing concerns about the reliability and generalizability of experimental results. In response, the ACM RecSys conference introduced a dedicated Reproducibility Track in 2020 to encourage rigorous, transparent, and repeatable research. This paper presents a structured analysis of the track from 2020 to 2025, covering 51 accepted papers. We classify contributions by type and analyze common patterns in datasets, algorithms, frameworks, and evaluation practices, with the goal of understanding how reproducibility is operationalized in practice within the community. Our findings reveal three main trends. First, the track has expanded in scope, evolving from a focus on reproduction and replication to include benchmarking, resources, and methodological contributions. Second, reproducibility papers exhibit a consistent methodological profile, relying on a limited set of datasets, algorithms, and evaluation protocols. Third, reproducibility in practice often involves extending prior experiments rather than strictly replicating them, with studies frequently introducing additional models or evaluation criteria. Overall, reproducibility work has improved transparency and documentation, but has had limited impact on methodological diversity, highlighting a gap between the conceptual definition of reproducibility and its implementation.
