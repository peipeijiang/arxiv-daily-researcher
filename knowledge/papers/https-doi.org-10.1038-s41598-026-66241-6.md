摘要 推荐系统已成为电子商务的核心组成部分，通过提供个性化和高效的购物体验，简化产品发现过程、增强决策能力并提升客户满意度。其有效性依赖于用户数据的质量与可获得性，而这要求用户信任系统能够负责任地处理个人信息。已有研究表明，通用隐私政策在建立用户对系统的信任以及提升用户分享个人信息意愿方面效果有限。基于Web信任模型，我们开展了一项在线实验，考察不同框架的隐私政策声明（分别针对推荐系统的能力、善意或诚信信念）对系统信任、采纳推荐意愿以及向系统分享信息意愿的影响。结果显示，无论是隐私声明的存在，还是隐私声明中对信任要素（能力、善意、诚信）的特定强调，均未显著提升用户对系统的信任、依赖其推荐的意愿或向系统分享信息的意愿。这些发现表明，仅凭隐私政策声明，即便明确设计用于传递可信赖信号，也难以成为促进用户对推荐系统信任的有效机制。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Abstract Recommender systems have become a central component of e-commerce, delivering personalized and efficient shopping experiences that streamline product discovery, enhance decision-making, and increase customer satisfaction. Their effectiveness depends on the quality and availability of user data, which requires users to trust the system’s ability to handle personal information responsibly. Prior research has shown a limited effect of generic privacy policies on creating user trust in the system and on users’ willingness to share personal information. Building on the Web Trust Model, we conducted an online experiment examining the effect of differently framed privacy policy statements, targeting the beliefs of competence, benevolence, or integrity of a recommender system, on trust in the system as well as willingness to follow recommendations and to share information with the system. The results showed that neither the presence of a privacy statement nor the specific emphasis on trust components (competence, benevolence, integrity) in a privacy statement significantly increased users’ trust in the system, their willingness to rely on its recommendations, or their willingness to share information with the system. These findings suggest that privacy policy statements alone, even when explicitly designed to signal trustworthiness, are unlikely to serve as effective mechanisms for fostering trust in recommender systems.
