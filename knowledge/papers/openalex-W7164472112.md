购物车遗弃仍是数字商务中持续存在的挑战，尤其是在购物者在结账附近遇到运费时尤为突出。本研究提出了一种阈值感知的结账优化系统，将购物车完成重新定义为多目标决策支持问题。该系统以目标梯度理论、多目标推荐系统和可解释人工智能为基础，推荐个性化的附加商品，帮助购物者达到免运费阈值，同时平衡消费者适配度与零售商侧约束（如库存和利润优先级）。利用真实零售交易数据，我们通过重构接近阈值的购物车进行了离线结构评估。结果表明，与静态附加商品推荐器相比，所提出的多目标决策支持系统在结构上提升了阈值完成可行性，尽管这种提升伴随着历史共现对齐方面的权衡。本初步研究确立了技术逻辑和结构可行性，而非行为或转化效果。未来的现场验证将考察信任、解释质量、决策质量以及实际的购物车完成情况。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Cart abandonment remains a persistent challenge in digital commerce, particularly when shoppers encounter shipping costs near checkout. This study proposes a threshold-aware checkout optimization system that reframes cart completion as a multi-objective decision-support problem. Grounded in goal-gradient theory, multi-objective recommender systems, and explainable AI, the system recommends personalized add-on items that help shoppers reach free-shipping thresholds while balancing consumer fit and retailer-side constraints such as inventory and margin priorities. Using real-world retail transaction data, we conduct an offline structural evaluation with reconstructed threshold-proximate baskets. Results show that the proposed multi-objective decision-support system improves structural threshold completion feasibility relative to a static add-on recommender, though this improvement involves trade-offs in historical co-occurrence alignment. This initial study establishes technical logic and structural feasibility rather than behavioral or conversion effects. Future field validation will examine trust, explanation quality, decision quality, and actual cart completion.
